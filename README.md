## Question 1

For customer transaction records at Tonye Trust Bank Ltd., **Integrity** is the most important principle.

Integrity ensures that transaction records remain accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification. If transaction records are altered incorrectly or maliciously, it could result in incorrect account balances, fraudulent transactions, and financial losses.

However, **Confidentiality** is also important because customers' financial information must be protected from unauthorized access or disclosure.

**Availability** is important because authorized employees and systems need access to transaction records when required to process transactions and provide banking services.

Therefore, **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA)** are all important, with integrity being particularly important for maintaining trustworthy transaction records.

## Question 2

A **Threat** is a potential source of harm to a system or organization. In this example, attackers sending phishing emails represent the threat.

A **Vulnerability** is a weakness that can be exploited by a threat. The employee's weak password is the vulnerability.

**Risk** is the possibility that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause harm. For example, attackers could use phishing to obtain an employee's credentials and gain unauthorized access to company systems.

### Summary

| Concept | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Threat | Potential source of harm | Phishing attackers |
| Vulnerability | Weakness that can be exploited | Weak password |
| Risk | Possibility of harm | Unauthorized account access |

## Question 3

The security gap is that employees outside the Finance department can access financial records, even though access should be restricted to Finance staff.

An appropriate security control is **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**.

RBAC gives users permissions based on their job roles. Finance employees would be given access to financial records, while employees from other departments would not have permission.

This control ensures that only authorized employees can access sensitive financial information, reducing unauthorized access and data exposure.

## Question 4

Visible CCTV cameras installed at a company's entrance can perform both **deterrent** and **detective** functions.

- **Deterrent:** Visible cameras may discourage people from attempting unauthorized or criminal activities.
- **Detective:** CCTV can record activities and provide evidence that can be used to identify or investigate incidents.

The function of a security control depends on **how and why it is used**, rather than simply on the device itself.

For example, CCTV used to discourage unauthorized activity performs a **deterrent function**, while recorded footage used to investigate an incident performs a **detective function**.

## Question 5

The main security concern when an employee copies customer information into a public AI service is **unauthorized disclosure or exposure of sensitive customer information**.

Before submitting the information, the employee should consider:

- Whether the information contains confidential or sensitive data.
- Whether the organization's security policies allow the information to be submitted to an external AI service.
- Whether the information could be stored, processed, or exposed by the external service.

One way the organization can reduce this type of exposure is to establish and enforce a policy that prohibits employees from entering confidential customer information into unauthorized public AI services.

Employee training and approved organizational AI tools can also help reduce the risk.

## Question 6

Allowing guest Wi-Fi, employee computers, a backup server, and sensitive business systems to communicate freely on the same network creates a security concern.

If one system is compromised, an attacker could potentially use it as a pathway to access other systems on the same network.

The organization should use **network segmentation or isolation** to separate these systems.

### Recommended Segmentation

| System | Recommended Protection |
|---|---|
| Guest Wi-Fi | Separate from the internal corporate network |
| Employee Computers | Place on an appropriate employee network |
| Backup Server | Restrict access to authorized users and systems |
| Sensitive Business Systems | Place in a restricted network segment |

Network segmentation limits unnecessary communication between systems and can reduce an attacker's ability to move from a compromised system to other parts of the network.
